Introduction

Welcome to Sharada Child Care, a children's clinic in Hoskote run by paediatrician Dr Veenadevi S, MBBS, MD.

We look after children from newborns to teenagers: vaccinations, check-ups, treatment for common illnesses, and advice for parents on feeding, growth and development.

We aim to make every visit calm and reassuring, for the child and for the parent.

Clinical summary

What we offer at the clinic. When you book, choose one of these as the reason for your visit.

Vaccination

Routine childhood vaccinations on schedule, from birth onwards. Please bring your child's vaccination card to every visit.

Consultation

Check-ups and treatment for fever, cough and cold, stomach upsets, rashes and other common childhood illnesses, with advice on feeding and growth.

Observation

Short stays under the doctor's care, under 6 hours or longer, when a child needs to be watched closely or treated before going home.

Ear piercing

Clean, careful ear piercing for children, with advice on aftercare.

In an emergency

If your child has trouble breathing, has a fit (seizure), is hard to wake, or is badly injured, go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away. Do not wait for an appointment.
