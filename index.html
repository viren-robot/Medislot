About the clinic

Sharada Child Care is run by Dr Veenadevi S, a paediatric specialist caring for children's health and wellbeing in Hoskote.

We offer vaccinations, consultations, ear piercing, and observation for children, for stays of up to six hours and beyond. Book your child's visit online by choosing a day, a free time slot, and the reason for the visit.

We aim to make every visit calm and reassuring, for the child and for the parent.
